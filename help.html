Help Center

Find your answer quickly.

Learn how ACTUAL HOLDINGS accounts, services, orders, documents and support work.

How do I create an account?

Select Sign Up, provide the requested information and verify your account. Your dashboard becomes the place to manage requests, orders and messages.

How do I request a service?

Explore Services, choose the service you need and sign in when you are ready to submit a request. The request can then be tracked from your dashboard.

How does Shop work?

Browse products, review details, add items to your cart and continue to checkout. Order and payment status can be connected to your account.

What about my documents?

Documents are handled through authenticated areas and controlled access. Do not send passwords, PINs or other credentials through public forms.

How can I contact the team?

Use Contact for general inquiries or the authenticated messaging area when you have an active service or account.

How is access controlled?

The platform is designed around authentication, role-based permissions, server-side authorization and activity monitoring.

Send the ACTUAL HOLDINGS team a message and tell us what you need.
